Credits
-------

This pak is derived from the vCommunity Management Pack for VCF Operations. Its
metric and property names, account fields, scoring tables and content come from
that work. The adapter code is a new Java implementation.

  - Onur Yuzseven created vCommunity and its adapter.
    https://github.com/vmbro/VCF-Operations-vCommunity
  - Iwan "e1" Rahabok designed its dashboards, reports and super metrics, and
    continued the pack.
    https://github.com/e1-rahabok/VCF-vCommunity-Adapter
  - Brock Peterson contributed to the pack and wrote the blog series that
    explains it.
  - Mark Arbogast and Robert Mesropyan are credited by the vCommunity project as
    assisting Onur Yuzseven.
  - Dave Kaber, Alan Renouf and the contributor recorded as atm8119 appear in the
    vCommunity commit history.
